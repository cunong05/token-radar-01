How the value is worked out

  • Sector multiple — FDV/TVL assumption by category, 0.22–0.34.
  • Growth — 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150%.
  • Age — days tracked with no token, up to 4 years of credit.
  • Chains — up to +12% for a protocol live on many chains.

“Pre-token” means the snapshot records no token — it is not proof a token does not exist. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of live tokens are excluded: they are packaging, not a launch. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued.
